## What is QQ Omega?

QQ Omega is an <span className="pink-glow-text">AI scoring and ranking system</span> built to give crypto traders and investors a <span className="gradient-text">**stronger decision-making edge**</span>.

It takes large amounts of noisy information, including market data, on-chain signals, token design, fundamentals, and macro context, then structures and connects it into a set of <span className="pink-glow-text">clear, comparable scores</span> that update automatically as conditions change.

Instead of relying on a single chart, narrative, or metric, QQ evaluates the <span className="pink-glow-text">full picture</span> and shows where an asset is strong, weak, improving, or exposed.

You can use QQ Omega to answer one question with more clarity:

*"How strong is this asset, and how attractive is it for **my timeframe**?"*

### How the scoring works

Each crypto asset is evaluated across <span className="pink-glow-text">five connected dimensions</span>: **Fundamentals, Tokenomics, On-chain, Technical Analysis, and Macro & Market Regime**.

Behind every dimension is a swarm of <span className="pink-glow-text">specialized AI agents</span>, each focused on a narrow slice of analysis, such as team quality, unlock pressure, liquidity depth, holder behavior, momentum structure, or market regime shifts.

Each swarm produces a clear <span className="pink-glow-text">dimension score</span> designed to show one part of the asset at a glance. QQ then connects those dimensions instead of treating them as isolated signals. For a deeper view into the architecture behind each category, see the <span className="pink-badge">[AI AGENT SWARMS](/docs/ai/what)</span> section.

All <span className="pink-glow-text">dimension scores</span> are combined into a global <span className="pink-glow-text">QQ Score</span>: a 0–100 evaluation that evolves as new data and events unfold.

QQ Omega produces <span className="pink-glow-text">four QQ Scores</span> for different timeframes by applying different weights to the underlying analysis. Short-term views lean more on On-chain and Technical Analysis, while long-term views prioritize Fundamentals and Tokenomics.

This creates separate **Daily, Weekly, Monthly, and Yearly rankings**. The strongest asset for the next few days may be very different from the strongest asset for the next few years. To understand how timeframes and rankings are built and how to read them, see <span className="pink-badge">[TIMEFRAMES & RANKING](/docs/scores/tf-ranking)</span>.

The result is a side-by-side view of an asset's structural strength and current market conditions, helping you spot <span className="pink-glow-text">risks, opportunities, and possible valuation gaps</span> relative to your timeframe.
